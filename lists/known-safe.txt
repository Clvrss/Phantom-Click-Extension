# Phantom Click - known safe domains
#
# One domain per line. Lines starting with # are comments; blank lines are ignored.
# Write the main (registrable) domain, for example: example.com
#   - subdomains are included automatically (shop.example.com counts as example.com)
#   - country domains need their own line when they differ (example.co.id)
#   - you may paste a full link; the extension keeps only the domain
#
# What a safe domain does:
#   - it is not flagged as "Unfamiliar domain" (R-02) and its path is not checked for scam words (R-05)
#   - its name (5+ letters) is protected: lookalikes such as "examp1e.com" are flagged as Risky (R-04, R-06)
#
# After editing: open chrome://extensions, click the reload arrow on Phantom Click,
# then refresh the pages you are testing (Ctrl+Shift+R). Saved results are cleared automatically.
